Little Lantern Stories

Gentle, original animated stories for children aged 3 to 8 — a new episode every day on YouTube.

What we make

Short cartoon stories (about 3–4 minutes) starring Pip the fox, Tumble the elephant and Nana Hoot the owl. Every story is kind, safe and ends happily with a simple lesson — sharing, honesty, courage, patience, gratitude and more.

How it works

Our stories, artwork, music and voices are created with the help of AI tools and reviewed for children. This website belongs to a small publishing tool that uploads those videos to our own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API. It does not offer sign-in to the public and does not collect data from viewers.
